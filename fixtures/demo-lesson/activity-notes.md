# Pencil observation — simulated activity notes
simulated: true

## Page 1: activity
With an adult's help, put a little room-temperature water in a clear plastic cup on a stable table. Rest a straight, unsharpened pencil partly in the water. Look from the side at an angle to the surface.
Compare the portions above and below the water. Lift the pencil out to check that it remains straight.
Light from the underwater portion changes direction at the angled water-air boundary. This explains the apparent bend.

## Page 1: safety
Use a plastic cup, never glass. Use an unsharpened pencil without a sharp point. Ask an adult to help. Keep water away from electrical equipment and wipe up spills with a towel.
Do not drink the water or put the pencil in your mouth. No heat, chemicals, sharp tools, lasers, or sunlight experiments are needed.
