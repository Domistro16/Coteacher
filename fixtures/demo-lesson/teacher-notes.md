# Light and refraction — simulated teacher notes
simulated: true

These are hand-written teaching notes for a fake classroom lesson, not a record of a real class.

## Page 1, section 1: straight light paths
In a uniform medium, light travels along straight paths. A ray is a drawing of the direction light travels. Light does not always follow curved paths.

## Page 1, section 2: refraction
At a boundary between air and water, light changes speed. When it crosses at an angle, its direction changes too. This is refraction.
Light from an underwater pencil travels from water into air before reaching the observer's eyes. The underwater part looks displaced, making the pencil appear bent; the pencil itself stays straight.
A toy cart crossing at an angle from a smooth surface onto a slower surface turns as one side crosses first. This is only an analogy for the direction change: light is not a cart.
